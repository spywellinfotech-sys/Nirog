Delete your Yada+ account

You can delete your Yada+ account at any time, as a patient, doctor or facility.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Sign in to the Yada+ app.
  2. Patients: tap your name at the top, then Help and support. Doctors and facilities: tap Help and support at the bottom of the home screen.
  3. Tap Delete my account at the bottom and confirm.

By email

If you cannot sign in, write to spywellinfotech@gmail.com from the email you registered with, or give us the mobile number on the account. We delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, email, age, sex and profile photo.
  • Your prescriptions, notifications and phone notification token.
  • The personal details and reason for visit stored on your past appointments. Open appointments are cancelled.
  • For doctors and facilities: the listing is taken down and your verification documents and contact details are removed.
  • Your sign-in is closed. You can register again later as a new user.

What may be kept

Anonymised appointment records (without your name, number or reason) and star ratings may be kept so that doctors' records and ratings stay accurate, and as required by law.
