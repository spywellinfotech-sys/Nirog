Privacy Policy

Yada+ (doctor appointments, labs, hospitals, pharmacies and ambulance services). Effective 16 October 2026.

Yada+ helps patients find doctors and care facilities and book appointments, and helps doctors and facilities list themselves. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Yada+ Android app, the iOS app and the website.

1. Who we are

Yada+ is operated by Spywell Infotech. For anything in this policy, write to spywellinfotech@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

WhoWhat
PatientsName, mobile number or email (and the Google account name and email if you sign in with Google), age and sex, an optional profile photo, your city, your appointments (doctor, date, time, mode, reason for visit), the prescriptions your doctor gives you, and ratings or comments you write.
DoctorsName, speciality, qualification, experience, fees, languages, clinic name and address, photos, mobile number and email, council registration number, and verification documents (such as registration certificate and photo ID).
Labs, hospitals, pharmacies, ambulance servicesFacility name, address, city, opening hours, services and prices, contact person, mobile and optional WhatsApp or call number, licence number and verification documents.
EveryoneA notification token for your phone (so we can send alerts), the notifications you receive, and basic technical information needed to run the service. We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

Health information (your reason for a visit and prescriptions) is sensitive. We collect it only to provide the booking and prescription features you use.

3. How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in (one-time codes by SMS, email, or Google).
  • To show doctors and facilities to patients and to book, confirm, reschedule and cancel appointments.
  • To let a doctor see the details of a patient who booked with them, and to issue prescriptions.
  • To check the licence and identity of doctors and facilities before they are listed. Verification documents are private and are seen only by our review team.
  • To send notifications about your bookings and announcements from Yada+.
  • To keep the service safe, prevent misuse, and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show ads.

4. Who can see your information

  • Doctors and facilities you book with see your name, mobile number, age, sex and the reason you gave for that appointment.
  • Patients see a doctor's or facility's public listing (name, speciality, clinic, fees, city, public contact number). Reviews show only the first name of the reviewer.
  • Yada+ administrators can see accounts and bookings when needed to run and support the service.

5. Services that process data for us

  • Supabase hosts our database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Google provides Google sign-in and Firebase Cloud Messaging for phone notifications.
  • apitxt.com sends one-time codes by SMS to your mobile number.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.

These providers process data only to deliver their service to us. We share information with others only if the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and access is limited by account and role. Verification documents and prescriptions are kept in private storage. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in details to yourself.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. Appointment and prescription records may be kept for as long as needed for the doctor's and patient's records and for legal reasons. When you ask us to delete your account we remove your personal details and, where the law allows, your records.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Update your name, age, sex and photo in your profile.
  • Turn off phone notifications in your phone settings.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information, or to delete your account, by emailing spywellinfotech@gmail.com from the email you registered with, or tell us the mobile number on the account. We act on requests within 30 days.
  • If you are in India, you have the rights given by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, including to access, correct and erase your data and to complain if you are not satisfied.

9. Children

Yada+ is meant for adults. A parent or guardian should book on behalf of a child and enter their own details. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 18.

10. Not medical advice

Yada+ is a booking and listing service. It does not give medical advice, and doctors and facilities are independent of Yada+. In an emergency call 112, 108 or your nearest hospital.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: spywellinfotech@gmail.com.
